## Description
Raise Your Right Hand is a group experiment played in the real world.

Every day, a small, random few members are picked. Each one gets a single chance to ask another random member to raise their right hand, straight up, wherever they are. That member gets 20 minutes.

If someone out there sees a raised right hand, they tap "I saw a raised hand." When the sighting matches, the clock resets, and the app records how long it took.

How long can the group go without a confirmed sighting? Keep your eyes open.

- No username, no password, no profile. You just join.
- You only share your location for the 20 minutes after you are asked, and it stops by itself.
- Every sighting is recorded with its time and city, so you can see the history.
- A daily draw picks about 3% of members, so being picked is rare and a little thrilling.
- The sky behind the clock matches the weather and time of day where you are.

Notifications tell you when you are picked or asked. Raise your hand, hold it up, and see who notices.

## App Privacy questionnaire (answers)
Tracking: No. Nothing is used to track you across other companies' apps or sites.
Data collected, all linked to an anonymous random ID, used only for app functionality:
- Precise Location: shared only for the 20 minutes after you are asked, and when you tap "I saw a raised hand".
- Coarse Location: the city and state of each sighting is shown in the history. The sky background uses your location rounded to about 11 km, and it is not stored.
- Identifiers (User ID, Device ID): the anonymous member ID and the push notification token.
- Usage Data (Product Interaction): sightings and requests.
Not collected: name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, health, financial info, browsing history.
