# Notes for App Review

Raise Your Right Hand is a group experiment. There is no account or login; the app creates an anonymous member ID when the person taps "Join the experiment."

HOW IT WORKS
Each day a small random share of members (about 3.36%) is picked. A picked member gets one chance, within 20 minutes, to ask a random other member to raise their right hand. The asked member has 20 minutes. Anyone who sees a raised hand taps "I saw a raised hand." If the spotter is within 50 meters of the asked member's latest location, the sighting is recorded and the "time since last sighting" clock resets.

LOCATION
- When In Use: the sky background (weather and time of day at the user's place) and the "I saw a raised hand" button.
- Always/background: used ONLY for the 20 minutes after a member is asked to raise their hand, so a spotter can be matched. It starts automatically when they are asked and stops automatically when the 20 minutes end. The app does not track location at any other time. A visible reminder is shown while it is active.
- Location is never sold or shared with third parties. Only the city and state of a sighting are shown to other members.

NOTIFICATIONS
Used to tell a member that they were picked, or were asked to raise their hand.

NATIVE FEATURES
Background location during the window, Apple push notifications, haptic feedback on a sighting.

HOW TO REVIEW
The daily pick is random (about 3.36% of members) and the game starts when 150 members have joined, so a review device is rarely picked on its own. Use the review code APPLE-REVIEW. It is in the app at the bottom of the screen: "App Review? Enter code".
1. Demo (screens only): tap "Start demo (screens only)". Buttons (Waiting, Picked to ask, Asked to raise, Sighted, History) show every screen. Demo mode takes no real action.
2. Live test with two devices (real asking, notifications, location, sighting): on BOTH devices enter the code and tap "Live test with two devices". Each device is then a review test member and is always picked. On device 1 tap "Ask a random member to raise their right hand"; the request goes only to your other review device. Device 2 gets a notification and a countdown: allow location and notifications and hold the right hand up (the app shares location only during the 20-minute window). On device 1, within 50 meters, tap "I saw a raised hand". A match message is shown. Review test activity is kept separate from real members and does not change the public counter or history.

There are no questions or answers in this app. "Ask" means asking a random member to hold their right hand up.

LAUNCH AREA
The game is starting in the San Francisco Bay Area. When a new member taps Join, the app checks location once and does not store it; people outside the area see a friendly "not open in your area yet" message. App Review can use the review code above, from anywhere, to see every screen or run the live test.
