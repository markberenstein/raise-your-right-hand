# Notes for App Review

Raise Your Right Hand is a group experiment. There is no account or login; the app creates an anonymous member ID when the person taps "Join the experiment."

HOW IT WORKS
Each day a small random share of members (about 3.36%) is picked. A picked member gets one chance, within 20 minutes, to ask a random other member to raise their right hand. The asked member has 20 minutes. Anyone who sees a raised hand taps "I saw a raised hand." If the spotter is within 100 meters of the asked member's latest location, the sighting is recorded and the "time since last sighting" clock resets.

LOCATION
- When In Use: the sky background (weather and time of day at the user's place) and the "I saw a raised hand" button.
- Always/background: used ONLY for the 20 minutes after a member is asked to raise their hand, so a spotter can be matched. It starts automatically when they are asked and stops automatically when the 20 minutes end. The app does not track location at any other time. A visible reminder is shown while it is active.
- Location is never sold or shared with third parties. Only the city and state of a sighting are shown to other members.

NOTIFICATIONS
Used to tell a member that they were picked, or were asked to raise their hand.

NATIVE FEATURES
Background location during the window, Apple push notifications, haptic feedback on a sighting.
