Privacy Policy

Raise Your Right Hand. Last updated November 2, 2026.

Raise Your Right Hand is a group experiment. This page explains what the app collects, why, and what you can ask us to do. It is written to be read.

No account, no personal details

You do not create a username or password and you never give us your name, email address or phone number. When you tap Join, we create a random member number for your device. That number is the only thing that identifies you in the app.

What we collect

Location. The app asks for your location in four situations. First, when you tap Join, we check your location once to confirm you are in the San Francisco Bay Area, where the game is starting; we do not store it. Second, to show the sky (weather and time of day) where you are. For this we send your location rounded to about 11 km to Apple WeatherKit for one lookup; we do not store it. Third, when you tap "I saw a raised hand", we use your exact location at that moment to see whether an asked member was within 100 meters. Fourth, when you are asked to raise your right hand, the app shares your location for the next 20 minutes only, so a sighting can be matched. It stops automatically at the end of that window.

Sightings. When a sighting is confirmed we record the time, how long the clock had been running, the location of the spotter, and the city and state. Other members see only the time, the wait and the city and state.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we keep your device's push token so we can tell you that you were picked or asked.

Activity. We record who was picked or asked (by member number) and the status of each request, to run the daily draw and the game.

How we use it

Only to run the game: pick members, deliver requests, match sightings, show the history and show the sky. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who sees what

Other members never see your member number or your exact location. Our hosting provider (Railway) stores the data. City and state names are looked up with OpenStreetMap using coordinates rounded to about 1 km.

Your choices

You can turn off location or notifications at any time in iPhone Settings, and the app keeps working with fewer features. You can ask us to delete the data tied to your member number by contacting us; to help us find it, send the member number shown in the app, or tell us roughly when you joined.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
