Raise Your Right Hand

How the game works

Each day a few random members are picked. A picked member gets one chance, within 20 minutes, to ask a random other member to raise their right hand. That member then has 20 minutes. If someone sees a raised right hand and taps "I saw a raised hand" within 100 meters, the sighting counts and the clock resets.

Common questions

Why am I never picked? About 3.36% of members are picked each day, starting at 9:00 a.m. Pacific. Asking is open 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific. The game starts when 150 members have joined.

I did not get a notification. Open iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then Raise Your Right Hand, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

My sighting did not match. The spotter must be within 100 meters of the asked member, and the asked member's location must be less than 5 minutes old. Make sure location is allowed for the app.

Does the app track me all the time? No. Location is shared only for the 20 minutes after you are asked, and when you tap "I saw a raised hand".
