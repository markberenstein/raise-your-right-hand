Hello, and thank you for the review. Answers to your questions:

1. Are users able to see other users' locations in your app?
No. The app never shows any member's location to another member. The server only compares locations privately to decide whether a sighting counts (the spotter must be within 50 meters of the asked member). The only place information other members see is the city and state of a recorded sighting.

2 and 4. How do users raise questions / add questions and answers?
The app has no questions or answers. It is a group experiment. Each day a few random members are picked. A picked member can ask one random other member to hold their right hand up; that member gets a notification and has 20 minutes. Anyone who sees a raised right hand nearby taps "I saw a raised hand" in the app. If they are within 50 meters of the asked member, the sighting is recorded and the "time since last sighting" clock resets.

3. We were unable to ask random questions in live mode with 2 devices in the same spot.
You are right, and thank you for pointing it out. The daily pick is random (about 3%), so a review device is almost never picked. We added a live test mode for review. On BOTH devices, tap "App Review? Enter code" (bottom of the screen), type APPLE-REVIEW, and tap "Live test with two devices". Each device then becomes a review test member that is always picked:
a. On device 1, tap "Ask a random member to raise their right hand". The request goes only to your other review device.
b. On device 2, allow notifications and location. It shows a countdown; hold the right hand up (it shares location only during the 20-minute window).
c. On device 1, within 50 meters, tap "I saw a raised hand". A match message appears.
Review test activity is kept separate from real members and does not change the public counter. The demo walkthrough (screens only) is still available with the same code.

Please let us know if anything else is needed. Thank you!
